Who We Are

Built in the Pacific Northwest

West Wood Design is a custom millwork and furniture fabrication shop based in Port Angeles, Washington. We operate out of a 1,080 sq ft heated workshop and build everything to order.

We specialize in solid hardwood furniture, custom built-in cabinetry and millwork, CNC routing, and laser engraving. Our work ranges from single statement pieces to complete room millwork packages and commercial retail installations.

Everything we build starts with a conversation. We work with you on the design, confirm the details before cutting anything, and deliver a finished piece you'll be using for decades.
